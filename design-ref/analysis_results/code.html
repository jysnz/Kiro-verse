Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

High Confidence 94%

Based on the visual analysis, the localized inflammation, erythema (redness), and textural changes strongly indicate a presentation of Eczema. This condition is characterized by a disrupted skin barrier, leading to moisture loss and increased susceptibility to irritants.

Observed Symptoms

  • check_circle

    Intense Pruritus (Itching)

    Often worsening at night, leading to scratching and further irritation.

  • check_circle

    Erythematous Patches

    Red to brownish-gray patches, commonly on flexural areas.

  • check_circle

    Dry, Scaly Skin

    Thickened, cracked, or scaly skin texture indicating compromised barrier.

healing

Immediate Care Plan

Moisturize Frequently

Apply thick emollients or ointments within 3 minutes of bathing to lock in moisture.

Identify Triggers

Avoid harsh soaps, wool fabrics, and sudden temperature changes.

warning When to see a doctor

If symptoms are severe, affecting sleep, or showing signs of infection (yellow crusts).
